# Privacy Policy — Sesh App

**Effective date:** 12 June 2026
**Last updated:** 12 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sesh App ("Sesh", "the app",
"we", "us") collects, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices you
have. Sesh is a social blood-alcohol-content (BAC) estimation and drink-tracking
app. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

> **Sesh is not a medical, legal, or safety device.** BAC values are
> Widmark-formula *estimates* based on the information you enter and are
> provided for entertainment and harm-reduction awareness only. Never rely on
> them to decide whether to drive or operate machinery. See the in-app
> disclaimer for details.

## 2. Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to function. Specifically:

### Information you provide
- **Account details:** your email address, used to create and authenticate your
  account.
- **Profile information:** display name, age, biological sex, and body weight.
  Sex and weight are required inputs to the Widmark BAC calculation; age is used
  to confirm you meet the legal drinking age.
- **Optional profile photo (avatar):** if you choose to add one.
- **Drink and session data:** the drinks you log (type, volume, alcohol by
  volume, timestamp), live sessions you start or join, and groups and manually
  added guests you create.

### Information collected with your permission
- **Location (while using the app):** used only to find and show nearby bars and
  venues. Location is processed to surface venues and is not used to track you
  in the background or sold to anyone.
- **Camera:** used only to scan beverage barcodes so a drink's details can be
  looked up. Images are processed on-device for scanning and are not stored or
  uploaded.
- **Push notification token:** if you enable notifications, a device token is
  stored so we can send session-related and group activity notifications.

### Information collected automatically
- **Basic technical data** necessary to operate the service (e.g. authentication
  tokens and timestamps). We do **not** use third-party advertising or
  analytics/tracking SDKs, and we do **not** use the Apple Advertising
  Identifier (IDFA).

---

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:
- create and secure your account and sign you in;
- calculate and display your estimated BAC and time-to-sober;
- power live sessions, groups, shared rounds, and the leaderboard/roast
  features;
- show nearby venues when you grant location access;
- look up beverages you scan;
- send notifications you have opted into;
- maintain, debug, and improve the app.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** use it for
third-party advertising.

---

## 4. How your information is shared

- **With other users you choose to interact with.** When you join or host a
  group session, limited information (such as your display name, avatar, and —
  within an active session — your estimated BAC and drink count) is visible to
  other members of that session, consistent with the social features you opt
  into.
- **With our hosting provider (Supabase).** Account, profile, and session data
  are stored in a Supabase-hosted database, protected by row-level security so
  users can only access data they are authorized to see. Supabase processes this
  data on our behalf as a service provider.
- **With Apple.** Push notifications are delivered through Apple Push
  Notification service (APNs).
- **When required by law,** or to protect the rights, safety, and security of
  users or the service.

## 5. Data retention

We keep your account and profile data for as long as your account exists.
Session and drink history is retained to provide the app's history features.
You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any
time (see "Your rights" below); we will delete it within a reasonable period,
except where retention is required by law.

## 6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/EEA GDPR and similar laws),
you may have the right to:
- access the personal data we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate data (most profile data is editable directly in the app);
- delete your account and personal data;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- request a copy of your data.

You can change profile details and avatar in the app at any time, and you can
revoke camera, location, and notification permissions in your device's Settings.
To exercise any other right, contact us at the email above.

---

## 7. Children

Sesh is intended only for users who are of legal drinking age in their
jurisdiction. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone under the legal drinking age. If you believe a
minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
