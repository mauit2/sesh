The flow

One app for the whole night.

Four screens, in the order a night actually happens — from the group chat that starts it to the recap that explains it.

01 · PLAN

Plan the party

Set up a party or a weekend trip, invite the crew, and let the supply calculator work out exactly how much to buy — from everyone's weight and the level you're aiming for, in bottles and cans.

02 · LIVE

Track it as it happens

Log a drink in two taps. Your estimated BAC climbs in real time, you can see when you'll be sober again, and the whole group stays in sync at the same table.

03 · NIGHTLINE

See who's out

Moments from everyone out tonight, in one feed. React, reply, slide into the DMs — the group chat of the night, writing itself.

04 · THE MORNING

Replay the damage

A full recap: peak BAC, the route you actually walked, every drink and schnaps, who was there — and how it all compared to the plan.
