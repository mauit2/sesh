The beer map, everywhere.

Zoom out and the whole world is on it. Every price is coloured against what a beer actually costs in that region and that currency — so cheap reads as cheap in Gothenburg, Lisbon or Tokyo, not just wherever the numbers happen to be smallest.

  • Any currency, anywhere. Prices follow the region you're in, and you can pick any currency in the world.
  • Filter by serving. 25, 33, 40, 50 cl or a pint — compared like for like.
  • Live deals from bars. Offers near you tonight, right on the same map.
  • Add a price yourself. From the app or the web — search the bar, tap the price in.

What beer costs

Beer prices, city by city.

We publish what a beer actually costs across Sweden — compared per centilitre, not per glass, because a cheap beer in a small glass isn't cheap. Updated from the same reports that feed the map.

All of Sweden → På svenska

  • Sollentuna is the cheapest city we cover. A median of 1.48 kr/cl against Stockholm's 1.90 kr/cl — about 29% less for the same beer.
  • Stockholm has the widest spread. 0.78 kr/cl to 3.48 kr/cl, so where you drink matters more than which city you are in.
  • 22 cities, both languages. Stockholm, Gothenburg, Kalmar, Malmö, Helsingborg, Linköping, Sollentuna, Uppsala, Umeå, Västerås, Örebro, Gävle, Norrköping, Karlstad, Lund, Växjö, Skellefteå, Landskrona, Jönköping, Sundsvall, Trollhättan, Halmstad.
