01The Application

Sejdel (“Licensed Application”) is a piece of software created to help people keep track of a night out, and customized for iOS mobile devices (“Devices”). Sejdel lets someone log what they drink and see a running estimate of their blood alcohol content, understand the pace they are drinking at, and get a rough sense of when they will be sober, so the night is something they can see rather than guess at. All figures are estimates calculated from what the user enters and are provided for awareness and entertainment only. It is used to log drinks as the night goes on and see a live estimated blood alcohol content, a drinking pace, and an approximate time until sober.

Furthermore, it is used to:

  • Start or join a live group with a share code, to see friends’ estimated BAC in real time, share rounds, and capture photos of the night;
  • Add friends, share a recap of a night, post stories, and optionally see which friends have checked in nearby;
  • Browse a map of beer prices and bar deals, and contribute prices;
  • Plan events and nights out with other users;
  • Play pass-the-phone drinking games;
  • Optionally connect Apple Health to see calories burned against calories consumed, steps, and heart rate for a night out;
  • For bars and venues, run a business account with a profile, posts, deals, and a guest list, on a paid subscription.

The Licensed Application is not tailored to comply with industry-specific regulations (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), Federal Information Security Management Act (FISMA), etc.), so if your interactions would be subjected to such laws, you may not use this Licensed Application. You may not use the Licensed Application in a way that would violate the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA).
