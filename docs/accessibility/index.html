Sejdel Website

Accessibility Statement.

Prepared: 9 September 2026  ·  Reviewed: annually

This statement applies to the sejdel.com website.

Found a barrier? Email contact@sejdel.com with “Accessibility” in the subject line, or use the contact form. We respond within 30 business days.

01Our commitment

Sejdel AB is committed to ensuring our services are accessible to people with disabilities. We aim to conform with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This accessibility statement forms an integral part of our Terms & Conditions.

02Accessibility features

You should be able to:

  • Zoom in up to 200% without loss of information or functionality;
  • Navigate the website with just a keyboard;
  • Listen to the website using a screen reader;
  • Stop moving images, by turning on the reduce-motion setting on your device.

03Conformance status

Status: partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

We conducted our most recent accessibility evaluation in September 2026. For more information, see how we test and non-accessible content.

04Non-accessible content

Below is a description of known limitations we’re working to address.

1 — Interactive map

The beer prices and deals map is provided by Apple Maps, and its pins and clusters cannot be reached or operated using a keyboard or a screen reader. The same information is available as text through the List button on that page.

2 — Display and text preferences

The website is presented in a single dark theme and does not provide built-in controls for changing colours, contrast levels, or fonts. The pages do respond correctly to browser zoom and to text and colour settings applied through your browser or operating system.

3 — Background animation

Some pages include slow decorative movement in the background. It stops automatically when your device is set to reduce motion, but there is no control on the page itself to pause it.

We are actively working to address these issues as part of an ongoing plan to improve accessibility.

05Scope of this statement

This statement covers the sejdel.com website. If you encounter an accessibility barrier in the Sejdel iOS app, please contact us at contact@sejdel.com and we will respond.

06Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 9 September 2026 and is reviewed annually, or when we make significant service changes.

We maintain records of accessibility feedback and our responses for regulatory compliance.

07How we test

We evaluate accessibility using:

  • Manual review of the website in September 2026 covering keyboard navigation, heading and landmark structure, accessible names for interactive controls, colour contrast, layout at 200% zoom, and reduced-motion behaviour.

08Feedback and contact information

Please let us know if you experience accessibility barriers using our services, or need information in an alternative format.

  • Email: contact@sejdel.com — please mark the subject line “Accessibility”.
  • Contact form: sejdel.com/contact

We respond to accessibility feedback within 30 business days.

09Enforcement

If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the enforcement authority in your country:

  • Sweden: Post- och telestyrelsen (PTS)
  • Germany: BFIT-Bund
  • France: Défenseur des droits
  • Ireland: National Disability Authority
  • Spain: OADIS
  • Italy: AgID

For other EU countries, contact your national consumer protection authority.
