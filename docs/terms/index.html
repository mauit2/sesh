Sejdel is an iPhone app for nights out. You log the drinks you have and Sejdel estimates your blood alcohol concentration from them. You can see where your friends are drinking, share photos and recaps of a night with them, plan events, find bars, beer prices, deals and sunny terraces on a map, and play drinking games with a group. Bars and venues can use Sejdel for Business to publish a profile, posts and deals to people nearby. Sejdel is intended for adults of legal drinking age. Sejdel is a fun BAC estimate, not a legal or medical reference. Never use it to decide whether to drive.

Sejdel is not a medical, legal, or safety device. BAC figures are Widmark-formula estimates based on what you enter, for awareness and entertainment only. Never rely on them to decide whether to drive or operate machinery.

29Alcohol, BAC estimates and your safety

Sejdel estimates your blood alcohol concentration (BAC) from the drinks you log, using a standard formula (Widmark) and the age, sex and weight you entered. It is an estimate, not a measurement. Real BAC depends on food, health, medication, how fast you drank, the true strength and size of each drink, and much more, and none of that is measured by the app. The estimate, the “clear” time and every other figure the app shows can be wrong, and can be wrong in the direction that makes you feel safer than you are.

Sejdel is a fun BAC estimate, not a legal or medical reference. Never use it to decide whether to drive. Do not use it to decide whether you are under a legal limit, or whether it is safe to cycle, swim, operate machinery, go to work, take medication, or drink more. The only reliable way to know your BAC is a breath or blood test. If in doubt, do not drive.

You are responsible for your own drinking and for the decisions you make while drinking. Sejdel does not encourage you to drink, to drink more, or to drink faster, and nothing in the app is a recommendation to do so. If you are worried about your drinking, talk to a doctor or contact a local support service, such as Alkohollinjen in Sweden.
