Sejdel is a mobile app and companion website for people who go out. It lets you log your drinks and see a real-time, estimated blood alcohol content (BAC), pace your night, and get a rough time-to-sober. You can start or join a live group with a share code to see friends' BAC in real time, share rounds, and capture photos of the night; add friends and optionally see who's checked in nearby; browse a map of beer prices and bar deals; plan events; and optionally connect Apple Health to view calories burned versus consumed, steps, and heart rate for a night out.

All BAC figures are Widmark-based estimates, provided for awareness and entertainment only. Sejdel is not a medical, legal, or safety device and must never be used to decide whether it is safe to drive or operate machinery.
