03Community guidelines

Sejdel is for people who go out together, and it works because people trust each other with a fairly personal picture of their night. These rules keep it that way.

Look after each other. Never use Sejdel to pressure, dare, or compete anyone into drinking more than they want to. Racing, forfeits, and “keep up” challenges are not what the numbers are for.

Other people's nights are theirs. Only post photos of people who are happy to be posted. Someone who has been drinking cannot meaningfully agree to a photo going up, so if in doubt, do not post it. Remove it if they ask.

Do not share what you see about someone else. A friend's estimated blood alcohol content, their location, whether they are out, and who they are with are shared with you inside a session, not with anyone outside it. Do not screenshot or repost it.

Be accurate about bars. Beer prices and venue details you add are used by other people to decide where to go. Do not submit prices you have not seen, and do not report a venue falsely.

Do not impersonate. Do not pretend to be another person, or claim a bar or venue you have no authority over.

No harassment. No abuse, threats, hate, sexual harassment, or unwanted contact, in posts, comments, or direct messages. Do not use the guest list or messaging to pester a venue or a person.

Nothing illegal, and nothing that puts people at risk. No drugs, weapons, stolen goods, or sexual content. Do not encourage anyone to drink and drive.

You must be 18 or over to use Sejdel.

If something is wrong, report it. Use the report and block tools in the app, or email contact@sejdel.com. We remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules.

09Complaints and removal of legitimate content

If you consider that some Content or Contribution has been mistakenly removed or blocked from the Services, please refer to the contact details at the bottom of this document and we will promptly review our decision to remove such Content or Contribution. The Content or Contribution may stay “down” whilst we conduct the review process.

Sejdel does not review content before it is published. Posts, stories, comments, submitted beer prices, and venue offers appear immediately.

We monitor in two ways. Any user can block another user, which takes effect at once and needs no decision from us. Any user can also report a post, story, comment, profile, or venue listing, with a reason. Reports go to a queue that only Sejdel administrators can read.

We review each report against this Policy and our community guidelines. What we consider includes what the content shows, whether the person affected consented, whether the account has been reported before, and whether the content is unlawful. Where the report concerns a photograph of someone who did not agree to appear in it, we act on the request of the person shown.

Depending on the outcome we may take no action, remove the content, warn the account, restrict its ability to post, suspend it, or suspend a venue's business account. Serious cases, in particular anything involving a minor, credible threats, or the use of the app to track or follow a person, result in immediate suspension without a warning. Unlawful content is removed and reported to the relevant authority where required.

We aim to review reports within a few days. Stories expire automatically twenty-four hours after posting.

If your content is removed or your account is restricted, you may contest the decision by emailing contact@sejdel.com, and a person will review it.
