Common questions

What time is happy hour in Stockholm?

It varies by bar. In Sweden happy hour usually falls in the early evening on weekdays, but each venue sets its own hours and changes them often. We list which bars run one and what the beer costs — we do not publish the hours until they are reported, because a wrong time is worse than none.

Where is happy hour cheapest in Stockholm?

Brazilia Restaurang has the lowest happy hour price we hold, 35 kr for 40 cl — 0.88 kr/cl.

How much does happy hour save you?

The median happy hour price for a large draught (40 cl) in Stockholm is 39 kr, against 76 kr for an ordinary one — roughly 49% less.

How many bars run happy hour in Stockholm?
