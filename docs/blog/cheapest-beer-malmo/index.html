Frequently asked questions

Where is beer cheapest in Malmö?

Surf Shack has the lowest price reported to us, 39 kr for 40 cl. Per centilitre, Surf Shack is best value at 0.97 kr/cl.

How much is a beer in Malmö?

The median for a 40 cl draught is 78 kr, and the cheapest we have seen is 39 kr.

What is the price per litre of beer in Malmö?

The median works out at 150 kr per litre, or 1.50 kr/cl. The best rate in the city is 98 kr/l.

Why compare per centilitre?

Because glass sizes differ. A 33 cl at 49 kr costs more per unit of beer than a 50 cl at 65 kr, even though the menu price is lower. Per centilitre makes the comparison fair.

How often are prices updated?

The page is rebuilt from the latest reports — this version is from 13 August 2026. We show the median of recent reports per bar and size rather than the most recent price.

Can I report a price myself?

Yes. Find the bar on the beer map and add the price, or do it in the Sesh app. It shows on the map immediately and on this page the next time it is built.

How we work it out

Prices come from Sesh users in the app and on the beer map. For each bar and size we show the median of recent reports rather than the latest one, so a single mistyped price cannot swing a table. Only prices in kronor, in sizes we can convert per centilitre, are included; a pint is treated as 56.8 cl.

Prices change, and happy hour is not counted. If something looks wrong you can report it on the map, which updates this page the next time it is built.
