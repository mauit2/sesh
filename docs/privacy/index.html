This Privacy Notice for Sejdel (“we,” “us,” or “our”) describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share (“process”) your personal information when you use our services (“Services”), including when you:

  • Visit our website at sejdel.com, or any website of ours that links to this Privacy Notice;

  • Use Sejdel — a mobile app for people who go out. It lets you log your drinks and see a real-time, estimated blood alcohol content (BAC), pace your night, and get a rough time-to-sober. You can start or join a live group with a share code to see friends' BAC in real time, share rounds, and capture photos of the night; add friends and optionally see who's checked in nearby; browse a map of beer prices and bar deals; plan events; and optionally connect Apple Health to view calories burned vs. consumed, steps, and heart rate for a night out;
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including any marketing or events.
Sejdel is not a medical, legal, or safety device. BAC figures are Widmark-formula estimates based on what you enter, for awareness and entertainment only. Never rely on them to decide whether to drive or operate machinery.

10Controls for do-not-track features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track (“DNT”) feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
