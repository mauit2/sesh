# Sejdel App Store shoot — image prompts

Paste each prompt into ChatGPT (GPT Image), Midjourney or Gemini. Save the result
with the **exact filename**, into `~/Desktop/sejdel-shots/`, then run
`upload-demo.sh` (or drag them into Supabase Storage). Every database row is
already pointing at these names.

**Global style, prepend to every prompt:**
> Photorealistic, shot on an iPhone at night, on-camera flash, slight motion blur,
> warm tungsten bar light, candid and unposed, Scandinavian pub interior, people in
> their mid-twenties, natural skin, no text, no watermark, no visible brand logos,
> no beer labels readable.
