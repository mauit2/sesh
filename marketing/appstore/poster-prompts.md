# Deal posters — 4 files, landscape 4:3, ≥ 1600 × 1200, save to ~/Desktop/sejdel-shots/

The app shows these as the venue's own promo poster on the map card, so they
should look like a bar made them: bold, a bit loud, one clear offer. No real
brand logos, no readable beer labels, no people's faces needed.

**Prepend to every prompt:**
> Photorealistic bar promo poster, landscape, printed-flyer look, high contrast,
> one big headline, Swedish krona prices, moody pub lighting, no watermark,
> no real logos.

- `deal-01.jpg` — **John Scott's Pub, "2 for 1 on large beer"**: two frosty pints
  clinking under warm pub light, big bold "2 FOR 1" headline, small "until 21:00".
  Deep red and cream palette.
- `deal-02.jpg` — **Brewers Beer Bar, "Tasting flight 99 kr"**: a wooden flight
  board with four small glasses, pale to dark, on a bar counter with taps blurred
  behind. Headline "TASTING FLIGHT 99 KR". Amber and black.
- `deal-03.jpg` — **Bar Himmel, "Wine by the glass 65 kr"**: one glass of red and
  one of white on a marble bar, candle, soft light. Headline "GLASS OF WINE 65 KR".
  Burgundy and gold, a little elegant.
- `deal-04.jpg` — **John Scott's Stable, "Free nachos with a pitcher"**: a pitcher
  of lager next to a loaded plate of nachos, flash-lit, fun and greasy. Headline
  "FREE NACHOS". Yellow and black.
